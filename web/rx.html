Input

Do not pick the same virtual cable for playback that you are listening on, or the decoded audio goes straight back into the modem's input.
Seconds of decoded audio held back before play-out starts. A dropout shorter than this never reaches the speaker — but you wait this long for audio at startup, and again after any underrun, and you are permanently this far behind live. The transmitter cannot help here: a live stream produces at one times real time and has nothing further ahead to send, so the only way to get ahead of a broadcast is to decline to play the beginning of it.
